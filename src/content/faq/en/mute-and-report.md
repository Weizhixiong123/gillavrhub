## Two ways to report

- **In the lobby:** mute or report anyone from the **leaderboard**.
- **Meta menu:** report from the headset's **Meta menu**.

## What breaks the rules

The terms of service forbid, among other things: harassment, bullying and threats; hate speech and slurs; sexual content; sharing personal information or asking for it; scams and real-money trading; advertising; pretending to be a moderator or Play Gilla; and cheats, hacks or mod menus. These rules apply to voice chat, names and the Discord.

## What happens

Play Gilla and its moderators act on reports. They can warn, mute, kick or ban, for a while or permanently, and remove coins, items or progress. If you think a ban is wrong, email support. See [contact support](/faq/contact-support/).
