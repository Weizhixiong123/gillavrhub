GILLA has three modes. **Roam (The Base)** is the main game: explore, find valuables and bring them to the recycler. **Hunt (Be the Monster)** turns one player into the monster. **Survive (Don't Splash)** is a race across a flooded hall on sinking platforms.

Use the finder above to filter by mode and see which monster hunts you there. Each mode is explained below.

## Roam: The Base

Explore with other players, find what's worth carrying and get it back to the recycler. The [Grinner](/guides/monsters/#the-grinner) hunts here and comes when you talk. There's a [keycard on a desk, a locked door](/guides/recycler-and-keycard/#the-keycard-and-the-locked-door), a vault and more underneath, where the [Mannequin](/guides/monsters/#the-mannequin) hunts. Start with [the Base map](/guides/the-base-map/).

## Hunt: Be the Monster

Vote for a map. One player becomes the monster, and everyone else has two minutes. Sonar pings show everyone through the walls. Caught players watch the rest on the monster cam. See [Be the Monster](/guides/be-the-monster/).

## Survive: Don't Splash

Cross a flooded hall on platforms that sink once you've stood on them. Touch the water and the [Mako](/guides/monsters/#the-mako) comes for you. Up to seven rounds, and the clock gets shorter every time. See [Don't Splash](/guides/dont-splash/).
