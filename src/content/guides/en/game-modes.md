GILLA has three modes. **Roam (The Base)** is the main game: explore, find valuables and bring them to the recycler. **Hunt (Be the Monster)** turns one player into the monster. **Survive (Don't Splash)** is a race across a flooded hall on sinking platforms.

| | Roam | Hunt | Survive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Official name | The Base | Be the Monster | Don't Splash |
| Goal | Bring gold bars and diamonds to the recycler | Monster catches players; players stay hidden | Cross without touching the water |
| Threat | The Grinner, the Mannequin | A player monster | The Mako |
| Map | The Base and the levels under it | Chosen by vote | A flooded hall |
| Rounds | Not stated officially | Two minutes for the other players | Up to seven, each with a shorter clock |

## Roam: The Base

Explore with other players, find what's worth carrying and get it back to the recycler. There's a keycard on a desk, a locked door, a vault and more underneath. Start with [the Base map](/guides/the-base-map/).

## Hunt: Be the Monster

Vote for a map. One player becomes the monster, and everyone else has two minutes. Sonar pings show everyone through the walls. Caught players watch the rest on the monster cam. See [Be the Monster](/guides/be-the-monster/).

## Survive: Don't Splash

Cross a flooded hall on platforms that sink once you've stood on them. Touch the water and the Mako comes for you. Up to seven rounds, and the clock gets shorter every time. See [Don't Splash](/guides/dont-splash/).
