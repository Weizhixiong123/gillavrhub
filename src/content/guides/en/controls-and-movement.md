In GILLA your arms are your legs. There is no walking with a thumbstick. You get around the way an ape does: push off the floor, shove off the walls and swing harder to go faster. The game uses **Touch controllers** and supports **standing** and **roomscale** play.

## How movement works

The official site describes three things you do with your hands:

- **Push off the floor** to move forward.
- **Shove off the walls** to change direction, climb and jump.
- **Swing harder to go faster.** Speed comes from how hard you swing, not from a button.

When a monster is behind you, your arms are the only way out, so practice in the lobby or a quiet corner of the Base before you go looking for loot.

## Tips for your first rounds

These are our suggestions, not official instructions:

- **Short, strong pushes beat big flails.** Keep your hands close to the ground and push back, like rowing.
- **Use walls in corridors.** Narrow halls are faster if you push off both sides.
- **Look where you want to go, not at your hands.** Your body follows your head.
- **Learn one escape route** from the recycler area before you go deep. If you have to run, you won't have time to think.

## Safety

The official terms of service ask every player to:

- clear a big space before playing and keep away from walls, furniture, people and pets;
- take a break at least every 30 minutes, and stop straight away if you feel sick, dizzy, tired or sore;
- keep the volume low enough to hear what's going on around you.

GILLA's comfort rating is **Intense**. See [comfort and motion sickness](/faq/motion-sickness/) for ways to ease in.

## Do I need a special controller or PC?

No. GILLA runs on the headset itself: Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S and Quest Pro. See [supported headsets](/faq/supported-headsets/).
