The Mannequin is **three metres of cracked, pale shell**, with **no arms and no face**. It hunts **under the Base**, down where, in the official site's words, "the rooms stop making sense."

## Where it hunts

On the levels below the Base. You reach them through the locked door, which needs the keycard. See [the keycard and locked door](/guides/keycard-and-locked-door/).

## What we know so far

The official site describes how the Mannequin looks and where it hunts. It doesn't say what draws it, how fast it is or how to escape it. We'll add that once we've confirmed it in-game, instead of guessing.

## Before you go down

- Drop off your loot at the [recycler](/guides/loot-and-recycler/) first.
- Go with your group and agree on a way back up before you climb down.
- Assume the general rule applies: monsters come to the sound of talking.
