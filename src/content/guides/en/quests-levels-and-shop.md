GILLA has **new daily quests** to claim and a **level** that everyone in the room can see on the **leaderboard**. Cosmetics, **limited items** and **bundles** are sold in a **shop you walk around in**. The game also has **coins**.

## Daily quests and levels

- **Daily quests:** new ones to claim each day.
- **Level:** shown on the room leaderboard, so everyone in the room can see it.

The official site doesn't list the quests or how much each level needs. We'll add them once they're confirmed.

## The shop

The shop is a place you **walk around in**, not a menu. It sells **cosmetics**, **limited items** and **bundles**.

## Coins and purchases

The terms of service say:

- coins, cosmetics and other items are **licensed to you for use in the game**. You don't own them, they aren't money and they can't be sold, traded or cashed out;
- purchases go through the **Meta Horizon Store** and follow its payment and refund rules;
- Play Gilla can change, rebalance or remove items and prices;
- if your account is banned, you may lose your items;
- faking, copying or generating coins, items or purchases is against the rules.

## Your ape's look

You choose your **fur colour** on the **lobby computer**, and everyone sees it. See [how to change your fur colour](/guides/change-fur-colour/).
