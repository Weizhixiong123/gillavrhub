There are **no official GILLA redeem codes, promo codes or free-coin codes** as of November 24, 2026. The codes you'll actually use in GILLA are **room codes**: you type one on the **lobby computer** to play privately with friends.

## Redeem and promo codes {#redeem-codes}

We checked the official GILLA website, its terms of service and the Meta Quest store listing on November 24, 2026. None of them mention redeem codes, promo codes or gift codes, or a place in the game to enter one.

So any site listing "GILLA codes" for free coins or cosmetics is not using anything from Play Gilla. If Play Gilla releases real codes, we'll list them here with the date and where they were announced.

## Room codes {#room-codes}

Room codes are how you play with friends instead of strangers:

1. Go to the **lobby computer**.
2. **Type a code** and share it with your friends.
3. Your friends type the **same code** on their lobby computer.

To play with strangers instead, jump straight into a busy public room. More in [play with friends](/guides/play-with-friends/#private-room).

## Fake codes and free coins {#fake-codes}

Our suggestions:

- **Don't download anything that promises free coins, codes or mod menus.** Downloads like these are a common way to spread malware.
- **Never give your Meta account details to a "code" site.** Play Gilla doesn't need them, and neither does any real code.
- **Know what the terms say.** Faking, copying or generating coins, items or purchases breaks the terms of service and can get you banned. See [cheats and mods](/faq/#cheats-and-mods).

## Where to check for new codes {#where-to-check}

If codes ever appear, they'll come from Play Gilla. Watch the [official Discord](https://discord.gg/gilla) and the official site, and check our [news page](/news/), where we post confirmed changes.
