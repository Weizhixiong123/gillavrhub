To change your fur colour in GILLA, go to the **lobby computer** and pick a new colour. **Everyone in the room sees it.**

## Steps

1. Go to the **lobby computer**.
2. Choose your **fur colour**.
3. Your ape updates for everyone in the room.

## Use your colour outside the game

Play Gilla offers an **official player model** for Blender at [gillavr.com/player-model](https://gillavr.com/player-model). You can pick its colour in the Material tab, and **your colour code from the game works too**. You can use it for profile pictures, thumbnails, videos, streams and fan art, including monetised videos and streams, under its license. You can't sell the model or things made from it, share its files or put it in other games. The license only covers copies you download from that page yourself.
