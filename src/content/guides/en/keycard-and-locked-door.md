To open the locked door in GILLA, **find the keycard on a desk** in the Base, **carry it to the card reader** and use it. Past the door you can **climb down** to the levels under the Base, where the Mannequin hunts.

## Step by step

1. **Search the Base for a desk with a keycard on it.** The official site says only that it is "on a desk." We haven't confirmed which room yet.
2. **Pick up the keycard** and carry it the way you carry loot.
3. **Find the card reader** next to the locked door.
4. **Use the keycard at the reader** to open the door.
5. **Decide whether to climb down.** The official wording is "Then climb down, if you want to."

## Before you go through

Our suggestions:

- **Drop off your loot first.** Bring what you're carrying back to the [recycler](/guides/loot-and-recycler/) before you go down.
- **Go with your group.** The site's own line is "Bring people. It's worse together," and voice chat carries from your ape's mouth in 3D. A group that keeps quiet can still give each other signals by pointing.
- **Expect the Mannequin.** It hunts down below. See [the Mannequin](/guides/the-mannequin/).

## What's behind the door?

The official site mentions a **vault** and "more underneath" as part of the Base. We'll describe what's behind the door once we've confirmed it in-game. See [the Base map](/guides/the-base-map/) for everything confirmed so far.
