In GILLA your arms are your legs. There is no walking with a thumbstick. Like other gorilla and ape-movement VR games on Meta Quest, you get around with your hands: push off the floor, shove off the walls and swing harder to go faster. The game uses **Touch controllers** and supports **standing** and **roomscale** play. Its comfort rating is **Intense**.

## How movement works

The official site describes three things you do with your hands:

- **Push off the floor** to move forward.
- **Shove off the walls** to change direction, climb and jump.
- **Swing harder to go faster.** Speed comes from how hard you swing, not from a button.

When a monster is behind you, your arms are the only way out. The [Grinner](/guides/monsters/#the-grinner) comes when you talk near it, so you may have to move fast and quietly at the same time. Practice in the lobby or a quiet corner of the Base before you go looking for loot.

## Tips for your first rounds

These are our suggestions, not official instructions:

- **Short, strong pushes beat big flails.** Keep your hands close to the ground and push back, like rowing.
- **Use walls in corridors.** Narrow halls are faster if you push off both sides.
- **Look where you want to go, not at your hands.** Your body follows your head.
- **Learn one escape route** from the [recycler](/guides/recycler-and-keycard/#the-recycler) before you go deep. If you have to run, you won't have time to think.

Movement matters most in [Don't Splash](/guides/dont-splash/), where you jump between sinking platforms and a harder swing means a longer jump.

## Safety

The official terms of service ask every player to:

- clear a big space before playing and keep away from walls, furniture, people and pets, because you'll be swinging your arms fast;
- take a break at least every 30 minutes, and stop straight away if you feel sick, dizzy, tired or sore;
- keep the volume low enough to hear what's going on around you;
- ask a doctor before playing if you or anyone in your family has had a seizure or blackout from flashing lights.

## Motion sickness

It can happen. GILLA's comfort rating is **Intense**: you move fast by swinging your arms. These are general VR tips, not official instructions:

- start with short sessions and stop **before** you feel unwell, not after;
- play in a cool room, and keep a fan pointed at you if you can;
- try the lobby before a full round of [Don't Splash](/guides/dont-splash/), which is the fastest mode;
- sit out a round if a chase leaves you dizzy.

## Do I need a special controller or PC?

No. GILLA runs on the headset itself: Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S and Quest Pro. See [supported headsets](/guides/how-to-get-gilla/#supported-headsets).
