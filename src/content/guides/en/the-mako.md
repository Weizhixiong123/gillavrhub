The Mako lives **in the water** in the **Don't Splash** mode. Touch the water and it comes for you at **24 metres a second**. By **round seven** it's doing **41**.

## Where it hunts

The flooded hall in [Don't Splash](/guides/dont-splash/). You cross the hall on platforms that sink once you've stood on them.

## Speed by round

The official site gives two numbers:

| Round | Mako speed |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 24 m/s |
| 7 | 41 m/s |

It doesn't list the rounds in between, so we haven't filled them in.

## How to survive

Our suggestions:

- **Don't touch the water.** That's what sets it off.
- **Don't stand still.** Platforms sink once you've stood on them, so plan your next jump before you land.
- **Plan for later rounds.** There are up to seven rounds, the clock gets shorter every round and the Mako gets faster.
