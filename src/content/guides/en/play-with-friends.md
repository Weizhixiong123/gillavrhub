To play with friends, **type a code on the lobby computer** and have your friends **type the same code** to join a **private room**. To play with strangers, jump straight into a **busy public room**. Voice chat is live and in 3D, and you can **mute or report anyone from the lobby leaderboard**.

## Private room

1. Go to the **lobby computer**.
2. **Type a code** and share it with your friends.
3. Your friends type the **same code** on their lobby computer.

## Public room

Jump straight into a **busy room** to play with other players.

## Voice chat

Voice chat is live, and your voice comes out of your ape's mouth in 3D for everyone in the room. That changes how you play:

- **Monsters come to the sound of talking**, so a talkative group is easier to find. The [Grinner](/guides/monsters/#the-grinner) in the Base comes when you talk near it.
- **Plan at the [recycler](/guides/recycler-and-keycard/#your-first-loot-run)**, then go quiet and point instead of talking.
- **Split up roles** before a loot run: one player carries, one scouts ahead, one watches behind.

## What to play together

- **[The Base](/guides/the-base-map/):** explore together, carry loot back to the recycler and open the [keycard door](/guides/recycler-and-keycard/#the-keycard-and-the-locked-door). The official site's own line is "Bring people. It's worse together."
- **[Don't Splash](/guides/dont-splash/):** cross the flooded hall without waking the [Mako](/guides/monsters/#the-mako).
- **[Be the Monster](/guides/be-the-monster/):** the room votes for a map and one player becomes the monster.

## Mute and report

There are two ways to report a player:

- **In the lobby:** mute or report anyone from the **leaderboard**.
- **Meta menu:** report from the headset's **Meta menu**.
