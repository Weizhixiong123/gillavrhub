In **Don't Splash** you cross a **flooded hall** on **platforms that sink once you've stood on them**. **Touch the water and the Mako comes for you.** There are **up to seven rounds**, and **the clock gets shorter every time**.

## The rules

- **The map:** a flooded hall with platforms.
- **The platforms:** they sink once you've stood on them, so you can't wait in one place.
- **The water:** touching it brings the Mako, at 24 m/s in the first round and 41 m/s by round seven.
- **Rounds:** up to seven, each with a shorter clock.

## Tips

Our suggestions:

- **Plan two jumps ahead.** The platform under you is already sinking.
- **Swing harder for longer jumps.** In GILLA, speed comes from how hard you swing. See [controls and movement](/guides/controls-and-movement/).
- **Don't follow another player onto a platform they just used.** It may already be sinking.
- **Save energy for the late rounds.** They're shorter and the Mako is faster.
