In the Base you hunt for **gold bars** and **diamonds** and carry them back to the **recycler**. That is the main goal of the Roam mode. Once you know the way, **find the keycard on a desk**, **carry it to the card reader** and open the **locked door** to the levels underneath. The catch is noise: monsters come to the sound of talking, so a loud group carrying loot is easy to find.

## What to collect

The official site names two kinds of valuables: **gold bars** and **diamonds**. It doesn't publish values or spawn points, and we haven't confirmed them yet, so we don't list any. We'll add locations once we've checked them in-game.

## Gold bars or diamonds?

The official site doesn't say what either one is worth, so nobody can tell you which pays more yet, and we don't guess. What you can plan around is the trip back. Our suggestions:

- **Something you get back beats something you don't.** A valuable only counts once it reaches the recycler, so pick up what you can carry home safely, not the most you can grab.
- **Short trips beat long ones.** You move with your arms, and the way back with your hands full is when the Grinner is most likely to find you.
- **Don't leave the carrier alone.** If one player carries, someone else should watch the route.

We'll add values here once we've confirmed what the recycler gives for each.

## The recycler

Carry each find back to the **recycler**. The official site doesn't say what the recycler pays out, so we haven't listed a reward here. GILLA does have **coins** (they are mentioned in the terms of service), but we haven't confirmed whether recycling is how you earn them.

## Your first loot run

Our suggestions:

1. **Agree on a plan before you leave the recycler.** Talking is safest when you're away from monsters. The [Grinner](/guides/monsters/#the-grinner) hunts in the Base and comes when you talk near it.
2. **Split up roles.** One player carries, one scouts ahead, one watches behind.
3. **Go quiet near the Grinner.** It is legless, drags itself on two long arms and comes to voices. Point at the route or the loot instead of calling it out.
4. **Know your route back.** You [move with your arms](/guides/movement-and-comfort/), so a long trip back with your hands full is when you're most exposed.

Playing with friends? Set up a private room first: see [play with friends](/guides/play-with-friends/).

## The keycard and the locked door

The official site gives the order in four short steps. In more detail:

1. **Search the Base for a desk with a keycard on it.** The official site says only that it is "on a desk." We haven't confirmed which room yet. The card is **gold, with the GILLA name and a black stripe**. The official screenshot shows one lying on a concrete floor, so if someone has dropped it, check the floor as well as the desks.
2. **Pick up the keycard** and carry it the way you carry loot.
3. **Find the card reader** next to the locked door. In the official screenshot it's a black box **on the wall by a hazard door**, the kind marked with yellow and black stripes, with a red light at the top.
4. **Use the keycard at the reader** to open the door.
5. **Decide whether to climb down.** The official wording is "Then climb down, if you want to." The site shows this step with a jagged hole torn through a storeroom floor.

What these look like is on [the Base map](/guides/the-base-map/#what-the-base-looks-like), with the official screenshots.

## Before you go through

Our suggestions:

- **Drop off your loot first.** Bring what you're carrying back to the recycler before you go down.
- **Go with your group.** The site's own line is "Bring people. It's worse together," and voice chat carries from your ape's mouth in 3D. A group that keeps quiet can still give each other signals by pointing.
- **Expect the Mannequin.** It hunts down below, and the official site doesn't say what draws it. See [the Mannequin](/guides/monsters/#the-mannequin).
- **Agree on a way back up** before you climb down.

## Mistakes that cost a run

Our suggestions, from how the Base is described:

- **Talking on the way back.** Plan at the recycler, then go quiet. Your voice comes out of your ape's mouth in 3D, and the Grinner comes to it.
- **Everyone carrying, nobody watching.** With your hands full you can't see what's behind you.
- **Going down with loot.** Drop it at the recycler first, so a bad trip below doesn't cost you what you already found.
- **Splitting up without a plan.** Agree on where to meet before anyone goes off alone.
