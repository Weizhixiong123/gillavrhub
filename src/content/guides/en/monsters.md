GILLA has three monsters. **The Grinner** hunts in the Base and comes when you talk near it. **The Mannequin** hunts under the Base. **The Mako** lives in the water in Don't Splash and comes for you the moment you touch it. In Be the Monster, a player becomes the monster.

## The rule that applies everywhere

Monsters come to the sound of talking. Voice chat in GILLA comes out of your ape's mouth in 3D, for everyone in the room. Talking is how you coordinate, but it's also how you get found. Plan in safe spots, then keep quiet near danger.
