GILLA has three monsters. **The Grinner** hunts in the Base and comes when you talk near it. **The Mannequin** hunts under the Base. **The Mako** lives in the water in Don't Splash and comes for you the moment you touch it. In Be the Monster, a player becomes the monster.

| Monster | Where | What sets it off | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [The Grinner](#the-grinner) | In the Base | Talking near it | Plan at the recycler, then go quiet |
| [The Mannequin](#the-mannequin) | Under the Base | Not stated officially | Drop your loot and go down as a group |
| [The Mako](#the-mako) | In the water (Don't Splash) | Touching the water | Stay on the platforms and keep moving |
| [A player](#a-player-monster) | Be the Monster maps | Chosen each round | Keep moving between sonar pings |

## The Grinner

The Grinner is a **legless ape** that drags itself along on **two long arms**, with its **mouth ripped open from ear to ear**. It hunts **in the Base**, the main Roam map, and it is drawn by voices: **talk near it and it comes**.

**What draws it:** talking. The official site says monsters come to the sound of talking, and that what you say on voice chat can get you caught. Your voice comes out of your ape's mouth in 3D, so it carries from where your ape is.

**How to avoid it.** Our suggestions:

- **Talk at the [recycler](/guides/recycler-and-keycard/#the-recycler), not on the way.** Make the plan before you leave, then go quiet.
- **Use hand signals.** Point at the route or the loot instead of calling it out.
- **Mute your mic** if you're the one carrying the loot. Your teammates can still hear the game. Muting other players is covered in [play with friends](/guides/play-with-friends/#mute-and-report).
- **If it's coming, stop talking and move.** Talking while you run only tells it where you are. A hard [arm swing](/guides/movement-and-comfort/#how-movement-works) is your only way out.

## The Mannequin

The Mannequin is **three metres of cracked, pale shell**, with **no arms and no face**. It hunts **under the Base**, down where, in the official site's words, "the rooms stop making sense." You reach those levels through the locked door, which needs the keycard: see [the keycard and the locked door](/guides/recycler-and-keycard/#the-keycard-and-the-locked-door).

**What we know so far:** the official site describes how the Mannequin looks and where it hunts. It doesn't say what draws it, how fast it is or how to escape it. We'll add that once we've confirmed it in-game, instead of guessing.

**Before you go down:**

- Drop off your loot at the recycler first.
- Go with your group and agree on a way back up before you climb down.
- Assume the general rule applies: monsters come to the sound of talking.

## The Mako

The Mako lives **in the water** in the **[Don't Splash](/guides/dont-splash/)** mode. You cross a flooded hall on platforms that sink once you've stood on them. Touch the water and it comes for you at **24 metres a second**. By **round seven** it's doing **41**.

The official site gives two speeds:

| Round | Mako speed |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 24 m/s |
| 7 | 41 m/s |

It doesn't list the rounds in between, so we haven't filled them in.

**How to survive.** Our suggestions:

- **Don't touch the water.** That's what sets it off.
- **Don't stand still.** Platforms sink once you've stood on them, so plan your next jump before you land.
- **Plan for later rounds.** There are up to seven rounds, the clock gets shorter every round and the Mako gets faster.
- **Practice with friends.** A [private room](/guides/play-with-friends/#private-room) lets your group learn the platforms together.

## A player monster

In [Be the Monster](/guides/be-the-monster/), the room votes for a map and one player becomes the monster. Everyone else has two minutes, and sonar pings show everyone through the walls.

## The rule that applies everywhere

Monsters come to the sound of talking. Voice chat in GILLA comes out of your ape's mouth in 3D, for everyone in the room. Talking is how you coordinate, but it's also how you get found. Plan in safe spots, then keep quiet near danger. More on voice chat in [play with friends](/guides/play-with-friends/#voice-chat).
