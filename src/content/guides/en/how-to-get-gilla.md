## Download and install

GILLA installs from the Meta Quest store like any other Quest game. These are the standard store steps:

1. **Open the Store** on your Quest, or the **Meta Horizon app** on your phone.
2. **Search for GILLA.** Check that the developer is **Play Gilla**.
3. Select **Get**. The game is free.
4. **Wait for the download** (about 1.07 GB for version 0.1.0). If you got it from the phone app, it installs the next time your headset is on and connected.
5. **Open GILLA from your Library** on the headset.

Before your first round, clear a big space: you'll be swinging your arms fast. Then read the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).

## Price

**Is GILLA VR free?** Yes — the game itself is free to download and play on Meta Quest. The in-game shop sells **cosmetics**, **limited items** and **bundles**. Purchases go through the **Meta Horizon Store** and follow its payment and refund rules. What the shop sells and how coins work is covered in [quests, levels and the shop](/guides/quests-levels-and-shop/).

You can't sell or trade what you buy. The terms say coins, cosmetics and other items are licensed to you for in-game use only. They can't be sold, traded or cashed out, and buying or selling anything for real money is against the rules.

## Supported headsets

GILLA runs on **Meta Quest 2**, **Meta Quest 3**, **Meta Quest 3S** and **Meta Quest Pro**, on the headset itself. You don't need a PC or a special controller: it uses the standard **Touch controllers** and supports **standing** and **roomscale** play.

Quest 2 is listed as a supported headset on the store page, so yes, it works on Quest 2.

The comfort rating is **Intense**, because you move by swinging your arms fast. If you get motion sick in VR, read [comfort and motion sickness](/guides/movement-and-comfort/#motion-sickness) before your first round.

## PC VR and Steam

**Is GILLA VR on Steam, PC VR or mobile?** Not officially. As of November 19, 2026, GILLA is only on the Meta Quest store, and the official site lists no Steam, PC VR or standalone mobile phone version. You can start the download from the Meta Horizon mobile app on your phone, or stream a Quest headset to a PC with Meta's own tools, but the game itself runs on a Meta Quest headset.

Watch out for fake PC or mobile APK downloads. The terms forbid modified, cracked or unofficial copies of GILLA, and players who use them can be banned. Only install GILLA from the Meta Quest store.

## Other platforms: Xbox, PlayStation, phone and Roblox {#other-platforms}

No. GILLA is only on the Meta Quest store, and the official site lists no other platform. It isn't on Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, iPhone or Android phones, and it isn't a Roblox game.

If you see GILLA offered anywhere else, it isn't the official game. The terms of service forbid making a copy, clone or knock-off of GILLA, and they ban playing on modified or unofficial copies.

## Release date and Early Access

GILLA was released in **Early Access** on the Meta Quest store on **November 10, 2026**. The first listed version is **0.1.0**.

It isn't finished. The official site says content is subject to change, and the terms add that Play Gilla can add, change or remove maps, modes, monsters, items and features. We note the version each page was checked against at the top of the page. Follow updates on the [official Discord](https://discord.gg/gilla) and our [news page](/news/).

## Age rating

GILLA is rated **Mature 17+** on the Meta Quest store, for intense violence and blood and gore. The terms of service set the minimum age as the game's store rating in your country:

| Where | Minimum age |
| --- | --- |
| United States | 17 |
| Most of Europe | 16 |
| Australia, Germany | 18 |
| Everywhere else | 17 |

Players under 18 (or under the age of adulthood where they live) need a parent or guardian to read and agree to the terms for them.

The terms warn that GILLA has blood, gore, jump scares, loud noises, flashing lights and dark places. It also has live voice chat with strangers, and you can mute or report anyone. See [mute and report](/guides/play-with-friends/#mute-and-report).
