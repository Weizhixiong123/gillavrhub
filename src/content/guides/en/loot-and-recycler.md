In the Base you hunt for **gold bars** and **diamonds** and carry them back to the **recycler**. That is the main goal of the Roam mode. The catch is noise: monsters come to the sound of talking, so a loud group carrying loot is easy to find.

## What to collect

The official site names two kinds of valuables: **gold bars** and **diamonds**. It doesn't publish values or spawn points, and we haven't confirmed them yet, so we don't list any. We'll add locations once we've checked them in-game.

## Where it goes

Carry each find back to the **recycler**. The official site doesn't say what the recycler pays out, so we haven't listed a reward here. GILLA does have **coins** (they are mentioned in the terms of service), but we haven't confirmed whether recycling is how you earn them.

## Carrying loot safely

Our suggestions for a loot run:

1. **Agree on a plan before you leave the recycler.** Talking is safest when you're away from monsters.
2. **Split up roles.** One player carries, one scouts ahead, one watches behind.
3. **Go quiet near the Grinner.** It is legless, drags itself on two long arms and **comes when you talk near it**. See [the Grinner](/guides/the-grinner/).
4. **Know your route back.** You move with your arms, so a long trip back with your hands full is when you're most exposed.
