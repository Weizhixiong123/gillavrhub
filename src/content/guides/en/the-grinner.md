The Grinner is a **legless ape** that drags itself along on **two long arms**, with its **mouth ripped open from ear to ear**. It hunts **in the Base**, and it is drawn by voices: **talk near it and it comes**.

## Where it hunts

In the Base, the main Roam map. See [the Base map](/guides/the-base-map/).

## What draws it

Talking. The official site says monsters come to the sound of talking, and that what you say on voice chat can get you caught. Your voice comes out of your ape's mouth in 3D, so it carries from where your ape is.

## How to avoid it

Our suggestions:

- **Talk at the recycler, not on the way.** Make the plan before you leave, then go quiet.
- **Use hand signals.** Point at the route or the loot instead of calling it out.
- **Mute your mic** if you're the one carrying the loot. Your teammates can still hear the game.
- **If it's coming, stop talking and move.** Talking while you run only tells it where you are.
